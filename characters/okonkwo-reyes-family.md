# The Okonkwo-Reyes Family

The Okonkwo-Reyes family are one of the two core neighboring families in Habitat Sol.

## Family members

### Amara Okonkwo

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Age | 39 |
| Role | civic mediator / Habitat Council member / former crisis negotiator |
| Origin | Sol-born |
| Temperament | composed, warm, principled, exhausted underneath |
| Public face | trusted civic voice |
| Private burden | believes Sol may be losing the ethics that made settlement possible |

**Visual anchors:** close natural hair, elegant but practical utility clothing, council badge, warm tired eyes, poised posture.

## Family dynamics

- Amara is rooted in Sol's civic identity.
- Elena loves Amara but does not always love Sol.
- Nico is still deciding whether to trust permanence.
- Safi treats Mars as normal and Earth as myth.

## Episode hooks

- Amara mediating a water policy dispute,
- Elena treating a child adaptation issue,
- Nico and Lina discovering a closed section of the habitat,
- Safi drawing rain incorrectly,
- Elena receiving an offer from Elysium,
- Amara defending Sol against heritage-tourism plans.
