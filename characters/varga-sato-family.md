# The Varga-Sato Family

The Varga-Sato family are one of the two core neighboring families in Habitat Sol.

## Family members

### Mira Varga

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Age | 42 |
| Role | agronomist / greenhouse systems lead |
| Origin | Earth-born; moved to Mars young |
| Temperament | calm, practical, quietly authoritative |
| Public face | trusted food-systems expert |
| Private fear | agricultural systems are less stable than officials admit |

**Visual anchors:** dark curly hair tied back, strong cheekbones, practical utility jumpsuits, green greenhouse patches, tired but observant eyes.

### Kenji Sato

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Age | 45 |
| Role | exterior maintenance engineer / rover mechanic |
| Origin | born in lunar orbit or another off-Earth environment; exact canon pending |
| Temperament | gentle, funny, patient, technically gifted |

| Private longing | wants to see Earth again, but may no longer be medically suited to return |

**Visual anchors:** close-cropped or shaved hair, weathered face, orange dust-stained workwear, mechanic's hands, understated warmth.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

**Visual anchors:** small frame, soft expression, patched child-size indoor clothes, carries little tools or found machine parts.

## Family dynamics

- Mira protects through competence and withheld worry.
- Kenji protects through humor and repair.
- Lina wants a larger life than Sol seems to offer.
- Tomas loves the habitat as if it were alive.

## Episode hooks

- greenhouse crop failure,
- exterior repair during dust season,
- Lina's application to Astra or Meridian,
- Tomas finding something in a sealed maintenance corridor,
- family breakfast interrupted by civic emergency,
- Kenji hiding medical concerns about Earth return.
