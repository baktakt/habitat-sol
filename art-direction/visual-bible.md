# Visual Bible

## Core visual direction

Documentary realism, soft cinematic light, lived-in habitat interiors, practical engineering detail, worn textiles, condensation, dust, hand-labeled equipment, and quiet human moments.

Avoid glossy generic sci-fi. Habitat Sol should look maintained, repaired, loved, and aging.

## Influences

- NASA documentary photography,
- practical realism from *The Martian*,
- social and infrastructure realism from *The Expanse*,
- melancholy intimacy from *Station Eleven*,
- quiet speculative poetry from *Tales from the Loop*,
- Scandinavian functional interiors adapted to scarcity.

## Lighting

- greenhouse: warm plant light, condensation glow, red exterior contrast,
- residential: soft low practical lighting, screen glow, warm domestic pools,
- civic spaces: layered institutional light mixed with improvised decorations,
- exterior: harsh sunlight, red dust, black sky, small human figures,
- medical: clean but compact, slightly old equipment, human softness.

## Camera language

The camera should often feel like a municipal archive, family documentary, or quiet editorial photograph rather than action cinema.

Preferred framings:

- medium portraits in lived-in environments,
- wide domestic tableaus,
- quiet close-ups of hands and tools,
- exterior long shots that make humans small,
- slightly imperfect documentary compositions.
