# Habitat Sol

> **Before Mars had cities, it had a town.**

Habitat Sol is the first permanent civilian settlement on Mars and the emotional center of the Habitat Sol universe.

## Basic facts

| Field | Canon value |

| Narrative function | pressure cooker, memory palace, small town, origin myth |

## Historical role

Habitat Sol began as the first permanent civilian habitat. It kept its original designation even after it became a real town with families, births, deaths, schools, rituals, politics, and local myths.

Larger settlements later surpassed it in population, wealth, technology, and influence. But Sol remains symbolically important because it was first.

## Civic personality

Sol is proud of its history, suspicious of polished outsiders, dependent on old infrastructure, and deeply committed to the idea that a settlement is more than a pressure vessel.

People from other cities may see Sol as quaint, obsolete, sentimental, or historically valuable. Sol residents often see themselves as the people who made everyone else's future possible.

## Built environment

The habitat should feel lived-in rather than sleek, but it is not dilapidated. Sol's survival depends on disciplined upkeep: pressure boundaries, seals, windows, life-support hardware, kitchens, clinics, and public spaces are clean, intact, and professionally maintained.

- modular components from different construction periods,
- intact panels with occasional specific, documented repairs rather than widespread patchwork,
- orderly equipment labels and old warning stickers,
- restrained condensation on operational greenhouse glass,
- family modifications beside municipal maintenance,
- narrow residential corridors,
- communal dining areas,
- soft fabric baffles to control sound,
- old civic murals,
- memorial plaques,
- limited red dust at exterior thresholds.

Age is expressed through design history, softened finishes, and continuity of use—not heavy rust, grime, cracks, broken fixtures, or a settlement that appears to be falling apart.

## Tone

Habitat Sol is not a dystopia. It is a town under constraint.

It contains affection, gossip, boredom, grief, beauty, work, small humiliations, local pride, teenage restlessness, civic rituals, and the constant knowledge that everyone survives because everyone else does their job.
