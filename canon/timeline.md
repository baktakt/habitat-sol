Habitat Sol’s weekly archive begins in **2187 CE / SY 93**. The first published episode is recorded as **Sol 7283**. `SY` means Settlement Year, counted from Habitat Sol’s founding in 2094.

## Founding era

The first three crewed settlement-shuttle attempts to Mars ended fatally for their crews. In Sol, the number **3** is painted or otherwise marked in public places as a memorial to those crews.

The first successful habitat-shuttle landing came in **2094 CE**, beginning the construction of Habitat Sol.

## Why this era works

By 2187, Mars has had enough time for Habitat Sol to become an old, inhabited town with civic memory, families born off Earth, aging infrastructure, and a distinct local culture. It also allows a wider Martian network to have developed: Meridian as an administrative and transport capital, Elysium as a wealthy metropolis, Ferrum as an industrial city, Astra as a research city, and Arcadia as an agricultural/water settlement.

This does not mean Mars is fully independent or self-sufficient. Habitat Sol’s survival still depends on deliberate answers about life support, water, power, food, medicine, transport, and Earth–Mars resupply.

## Open historical anchors

The following dates remain intentionally undecided:

- the sequence and dates of the other early Habitat settlements,
- dates of Mars’s political and economic separation—or continued dependence—from Earth.
