# Earth in 2187

**Status: established — creator decision.** This is fictional future history, not a scientific forecast.

Earth remains inhabited. Large parts of the planet have become uninhabitable because of climate change, causing large-scale population displacement and conflicts over habitable land and resources.

Wealth provides protection: rich people can buy access to gated communities and a reasonable standard of living. Middle- and lower-income people face increasingly difficult conditions. The consequences of climate change are shared unequally, not experienced as one uniform planetary collapse.

## Story implications

- Earth is still a lived-in world, not an empty ruin or a planet humanity has collectively abandoned.
- Climate displacement, class, access to safety, and inherited responsibility can inform family stories on Mars.
- Mars is not automatically a refuge accessible to everyone, nor a technological solution that erases inequality.
- A child's question about why earlier generations did not do enough can open an intimate scene. Adults need not have a complete, authoritative answer.
- Contemporary observations can inspire fiction, but specific future events, dates, migration mechanisms, and family histories must not be invented as established facts.
