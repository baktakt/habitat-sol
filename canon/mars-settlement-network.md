# Martian Settlement Network

Early permanent Martian settlements followed a naming convention created by engineers, planners, and bureaucrats:

> **Habitat + singular Latin-root or symbolic noun**

Over time, these designations became real place names. In daily speech, residents often drop "Habitat" and simply say Sol, Meridian, Elysium, Ferrum, Astra, or Arcadia.

## Canonical early settlements

| Settlement | Role | Population | Character | Nickname |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| **Habitat Sol** | first civilian town | ~2,000 | intimate, historic, worn, civic-minded | Sol / Old Sol |
| **Habitat Meridian** | administrative and transport capital | ~250,000 | efficient, cosmopolitan, controlled | Meridian |
| **Habitat Elysium** | largest and wealthiest city | ~600,000 | clean, fashionable, medically advanced, status-conscious | Elysium |
| **Habitat Ferrum** | industrial powerhouse | ~90,000 | tough, unionized, fabrication-heavy | Ferrum |
| **Habitat Astra** | university and research city | ~45,000 | intellectual, youthful, politically progressive | Astra |
| **Habitat Arcadia** | agriculture, water, and ice city | ~75,000 | experimental, self-sufficient, ecological | Arcadia |

## Social meanings

- **Sol** values history, community, continuity, and civic memory.
- **Meridian** values influence, logistics, law, and administrative control.
- **Elysium** values status, beauty, health, luxury, media, and biotech.
- **Ferrum** values solidarity, labor, durability, and practical power.
- **Astra** values knowledge, youth, science, argument, and ambition.
- **Arcadia** values self-sufficiency, water, food, ecology, and long-term survival.

## Everyday speech examples

- "She moved to Meridian."
- "He grew up in Ferrum."
- "The train from Elysium is delayed."
- "Astra kids think every argument is a thesis defense."
- "Sol people never stop talking about being first."

## Later naming evolution

Later settlements may move away from the Habitat convention and use more commercial, regional, or self-governing names, such as New Cydonia, Port Lowell, Mariner City, Elysium Central, Ares Junction, or Borealis.

This creates a generational divide: **Habitat** names belong to the heroic early era, while newer places sound more confident, regional, and market-driven.
