# Story Seeds

Fragments, images, situations, emotions, or questions that might become episodes.

A seed does not need an outline or ending.

## A child hears rain

**Situation**

Safi hears an old Earth recording of rain through the wall and thinks there is a leak.

**Emotion**

Wonder turning into embarrassment.

**Character**

Safi and Elena.

**Question or tension**

Elena realizes Safi has never heard uncontrolled water.

**No required ending**

---

## The girl next door

**Situation**

Nico suddenly sees his neigbour Lina in new light, from being the older close friend she has always been, to something more.

**Emotion**

Love, fear, curious

**Character**

Nico and Lina.

**Question or tension**

Can you be in love with a two year old older girl. Does she feel the same way? Will he destroy their relationship?

**No required ending**
