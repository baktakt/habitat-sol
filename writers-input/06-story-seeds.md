A seed does not need an outline or ending.

## A child hears rain

**Situation**

Safi hears an old Earth recording of rain through the wall and thinks there is a leak.

**Emotion**

Wonder turning into embarrassment.

**Character**

Safi and Elena.

**Question or tension**

Elena realizes Safi has never heard uncontrolled water.

**No required ending**
