## Example

### 2026-07-24

**Worked**

- The broken lunch container made the setting feel ordinary.
- Amara avoiding the political question felt true.
- The ending was understated.

**Did not work**

- Too much explanation of oxygen recycling.
- Everyone spoke too elegantly.
- Mars felt dangerous in a generic action-movie way.
- The final sentence explained the theme.

**General rule extracted**

Infrastructure should usually be experienced as inconvenience, work, ritual, or dependency rather than explained as technology.
