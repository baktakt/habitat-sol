# Human Truths

Short observations about people, relationships, work, family, grief, ambition, community, and belonging.

These do not need to mention Mars. They exist to make the stories feel human rather than generated.

## Entries

- Parents often experience a child's independence as both success and rejection.
- Old workplaces feel smaller when you return to them.
- People complain about small inconveniences when the real problem feels too large.
- A repaired household object can contain more family history than an expensive new one.
- Children misunderstand adults accurately.
- Communities praise essential workers while quietly making their lives impossible.
