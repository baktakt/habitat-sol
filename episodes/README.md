# Episodes

Habitat Sol publishes weekly fiction glimpses and world notes. The next format trial is a **Reel made from still images or restrained motion with original music**. Earlier carousel entries remain valid; do not convert or rewrite published work retroactively.

## Public copy

- **Fiction glimpse:** normally 120–220 words, one human moment or exchange.
- **World note:** normally 70–160 words, one established subject that helps readers understand life in Sol. Existing longer carousels are historical exceptions, not a requirement for future entries.
- Give newcomers clear bearings: who, where, and when. Leave room for emotional ambiguity, not confusion about family relationships or the setting.
- For Reels, pace on-screen text for comfortable reading; the full prose can live in the caption. Do not squeeze the whole story onto one frame.
- Interleave family stories with useful introductions to Sol, its history, and its people. Connect back to an earlier relationship, object, or question where natural.

## Minimal episode record

Keep what supports writing and continuity, not a mandatory production audit. New files need only an ID, title, entry type, status, relevant setting/characters, and public copy. Add story dates when known; publication dates are separate and must never be guessed.

```markdown
---
episode: 0004
title: Working title
entry_type: fiction_glimpse
status: draft
earth_year: 2187
location: Habitat Sol
characters: []
---

# Working title

[Public story or world-note copy.]
```

Optional sections, only when useful:

- **Image / music direction:** a short creative brief.
- **Continuity:** consequential new facts or open questions requiring a creator decision. Omit when there are none.
- **Publication:** known date and public link.
- **Assets:** a link to the selected media or external production bundle.

Do not add empty fields, `TBD` checklists, mandatory seeds, model settings, or collector metadata. Future automation should capture technical details in an external production bundle without asking the author to fill them in. Keep useful historical prompts already recorded; do not mistake them for verified final media.

## Status and author approval

- `draft`: a proposal, including automatically produced Friday packages.
- `approved`: the creator approved this version; not proof of publication.
- `published`: the creator confirmed publication or it was verified from the live post.

Silence is not approval. Material edits after approval require renewed approval. Never rewrite published prose merely to match new worldbuilding.
