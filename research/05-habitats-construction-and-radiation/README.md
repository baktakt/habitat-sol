# Habitats, Construction and Radiation

Reference material for making places people can live: pressure shells, underground or covered structures, regolith shielding, construction methods, materials, dust exclusion, thermal management, fire safety, and inspection/repair.

## Writer questions

- How are spaces built, enlarged, sealed, inspected, and repaired?
- What does radiation protection demand of the plan, schedule, and daily habits?
- What kind of damage is patchable, and what requires isolation or evacuation?
