# Life Support, Water, Air and Waste

Reference material for closed or partially closed habitat systems: atmosphere management, oxygen production, CO₂ removal, water recovery, hygiene, waste, spares, sensors, contamination, and failure response.

## Writer questions

- What is continuously consumed, produced, purified, stored, or vented?
- Which failure modes are slow, which are sudden, and which are invisible?
- How much redundancy, maintenance work, and reserve capacity make a settlement believable?
- Which resources are socially controlled because they are life-critical?
