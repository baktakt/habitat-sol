# Energy, Industry and Resources

Reference material for how a Mars settlement powers, heats, stores, mines, extracts, manufactures, and repairs its world—including solar and nuclear systems, ISRU chemistry, industrial bottlenecks, and waste heat.

## Writer questions

- What limits growth: watts, heat rejection, feedstock, parts, labour, or expertise?
- What can be extracted locally versus imported from Earth?
- How do dust, season, location, and energy storage affect ordinary life?
