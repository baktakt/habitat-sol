# Social Systems and Mission Operations

Reference material for the human institutions built around hostile constraints: work rhythms, crew operations, governance, law, economics, education, culture, privacy, conflict, and the psychology of dependence.

This folder should use scientific and historical evidence carefully. It supports storytelling; it should not turn research into a claim that any particular society is inevitable.

## Writer questions

- Who has authority over air, food, power, movement, and information—and why?
- What routines and rituals grow from maintenance, danger, confinement, and long communication delays?
- How do scarce resources create care, hierarchy, black markets, trust, or conflict?
